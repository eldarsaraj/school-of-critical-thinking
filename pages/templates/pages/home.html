Scientific approach to test prep

Reverse-engineered from the source.

Most test prep is guesswork. Tutors and prep centers work from experience and intuition, not from a systematic analysis of what each test actually measures. We do the opposite. Every test on this platform is built by reading the official materials carefully and working backwards from the answer rationales — using test theory, cognitive science, and the principles of critical thinking. What you get is adaptive tests, an intuitive dashboard, and a precision error analysis that shows exactly where your child should focus next.
