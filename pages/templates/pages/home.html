Critical Thinking Curriculum

Five foundations of clear thinking.

The curriculum is built around five things every young person needs to understand about how the world actually works: patterns, causality, uncertainty, models, and decision-making. It covers ages 8 to 16, one lesson per week, and fits alongside whatever curriculum you already use. This isn't a collection of puzzles or logic games — it's a structured program that teaches your child to notice what's actually happening, question what they're told, and make better decisions because of it.

Patterns

Recognizing structure in information — what repeats, what breaks, and what that tells you.

Causality

Understanding why things happen — and why "because it came first" is not a good enough answer.

Uncertainty

Getting comfortable with not knowing — and learning to act well anyway.

Models

Every explanation is a simplification. Learning to use models without mistaking them for reality.

Precision Test Prep

When your child can think clearly, test scores follow.

Every competitive admissions test is a proxy for reasoning skills. We reverse-engineer those skills from the official materials and teach them directly — through adaptive practice tests, detailed error analysis, and a scoring model that never flatters. The same thinking foundations that drive the curriculum also drive the test prep. Currently available for the SHSAT, with more exams coming.

Adaptive practice tests

Tests route the student to the next set of questions based on their performance, matching the real adaptive structure of the exams. Our scoring is conservative, so your child never thinks they're more prepared than they actually are.

Distractor trap analysis

Every wrong answer is tagged by the specific reasoning error behind it. After each test, your child sees which traps caught them and how often, so review is targeted, not random.

Accuracy by skill area

Broken down by section and skill so you know exactly which areas are costing the most points (and by how much) across every test your child has taken.

Built from official materials

Every question on this platform was written by reading the official test handbooks and working backwards from the answer rationales. Not guesswork, but a systematic reconstruction of what each test actually measures.
