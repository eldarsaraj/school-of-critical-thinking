Scientific approach to test prep

Reverse-engineered from the source.

Many places offer SHSAT prep, both online and in person. But, the thing is: nobody really knows what's under the hood of the test. The DOE doesn't publish the test blueprint, and the test itself is designed to be opaque. Most private tutors and tutoring centers are guessing at what is being tested and how to teach it. We're different. Our approach is nerdy, and proudly so. We reverse-engineered the test from the official DOE handbooks, and built our program using test theory, cognitive science, and the principles of critical thinking.
