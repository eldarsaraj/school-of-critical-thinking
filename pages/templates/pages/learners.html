For Learners

Whether you are preparing for a high-stakes exam, learning to code, or an adult who wants to think more clearly about the world: this is where to start. The tools here are built on the same foundations as the curriculum, applied to specific problems that learners actually face.

For students, ages 12-14

SHSAT Preparation

The SHSAT, New York City's entrance exam for specialized high schools, rewards exactly the skills the curriculum teaches: pattern recognition, logical reasoning, decomposition, and reasoning under uncertainty. Most test prep treats it as a puzzle to be gamed through rote drilling. This one treats it as an application of real critical thinking skills.

The dashboard includes full practice tests, worked solutions, and performance tracking tied to specific thinking skills rather than just right and wrong answers.

Go to the SHSAT Dashboard →

For children, ages 10+

Python Detective

An introductory coding course that teaches children analytical thinking through the programming language Python. Students learn to think analytically by writing real code, solving puzzles, and doing projects that require reasoning, not just typing. Free, browser-based, no installation required.

Start Python Detective →

For adults

Thinking Diagnostic

A free diagnostic that identifies the specific ways your reasoning breaks down. Not a general quiz. It maps your answers against 16 named thinking patterns and produces a personalized report showing where you are strongest and where you have the most room to improve. Takes about fifteen minutes.

Take the Thinking Diagnostic →
