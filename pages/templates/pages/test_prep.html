The approach

The same method, applied to every test.

Each test we build starts the same way: reading the official materials carefully and working backwards from the answer rationales. Questions are constructed according to latent variable theory, ensuring they measure what the actual test measures. Wrong answers are tagged by trap type — so your child learns not just what they got wrong, but the specific reasoning error behind it. No AI-generated content. No guesswork.
