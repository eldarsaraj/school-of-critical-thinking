The approach

Scientific rigor, applied to test prep.

Each test we build starts the same way: reading the official materials carefully and working backwards from the answer rationales. Questions are constructed according to latent variable theory, ensuring they measure what the actual test measures. Wrong answers are tagged by trap type, so your child learns not just what they got wrong, but the specific reasoning error behind it. Every explanation a student sees was written and structured by a person, and not generated on the fly by a chatbot.
