Who are we?

We're not a tutoring company. The School of Critical Thinking is a research and analytics organization that uses machine learning and NLP to reverse-engineer standardized tests — pulling apart question structures, score distributions, and difficulty patterns that aren't visible to the naked eye.

We don't sell test prep. We don't have a tutoring program. What we offer is the analysis itself, presented clearly, so you can make your own decisions based on data, not on marketing from companies that profit from your anxiety.

The briefing is led by Eldar Sarajlic, Ph.D. — Associate Professor at the City University of New York, published researcher on reasoning and education, and the person behind the data analysis you'll see in the session. This briefing is free.
