AXIS-5

Critical thinking is the degree to which your mental model has the same shape as the reality you're operating within. Nobody is uniformly good at it because it's a complex set of skills. For example, someone who reads a spreadsheet perfectly can still miss a confounder; someone who spots every confounder can still be badly calibrated about risk.

AXIS-5 is a critical thinking test that measures five of these separately — uncertainty, model awareness, causal reasoning, abstraction control, and trust distribution — in about fifteen minutes. It's a one-of-a-kind test, aimed not to measure your intelligence (that's a separate ability) but your practical reasoning skills.

Answer these questions honestly and without any external assistance (no googling or using AI). What you will get as a result is an alignment report: how well calibrated your mind is to external reality. Use the results as a guidance for your further development.
