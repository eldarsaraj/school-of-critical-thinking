AXIS-5

Most thinking errors aren't random — they follow predictable patterns. AXIS-5 measures five dimensions where those patterns appear: how you handle uncertainty, how you build and use mental models, how you reason about cause and effect, how you move between abstract and concrete thinking, and how much weight you give to your own judgment versus others'. The assessment takes around 20 minutes. You'll receive a detailed report.
