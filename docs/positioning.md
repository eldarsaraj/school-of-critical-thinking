# Positioning

**Who am I?**

I'm a 48-year-old philosopher, sick and tired of bullshit in education. I teach critical thinking. I started reverse-engineering standardized tests so parents would know how to actually prep their kids — I built this for my own child first.

**Where did I start?**

I grew up in war-torn Bosnia and came out of it as a refugee with nothing. My schooling was bad, and I stayed confused by education for most of my life. The war got me interested in policy — you pay attention when you've lived through what bad policy does. Clarity came when I taught myself statistics, philosophy, logic, math. My education didn't teach me; I had to do it myself.

**What do I believe?**

Anything can be reverse-engineered down to first principles and rebuilt from there. No matter how complex the skill, done this way, anybody can learn it. What learners need is the core underneath. Most of what's sold as education is fluff on top of it.

**Who am I doing it for?**

Parents who can feel that the prep industry is selling them fluff but don't have the tools to see through it. Learners who are tired of bad education and locked out of a better one. People who want to transform their lives — or their children's — through real learning.

**Why me?**

Because I can see to the bottom of things and sift the bullshit from the essence. That skill was forged the hard way: a rough life, a wide education, and having to learn everything myself because my schools failed me.

**Where am I going?**

Highly personalized test prep for everyone — analytics down to the atomic level, so every kid learns from exactly where they are. I'm building the company that gets us there: the most prestigious test analytics company in the world, the one that sets the new standard for what test prep education means.

**The payoff**

The brand and the community. I want people to think of the School of Critical Thinking as the highest-level educational company — the one that sifts through the bullshit and goes straight to the core of what matters.

**The end:** highly personalized test prep education for everyone.

**Belief 1:** Most educational frameworks are built for a median student that doesn't exist. They fail everyone else.

**Belief 2:** Everything can be learned when decomposed to first principles.

**Belief 3:** Everyone starts at a different place. Learning depends on specifying that place.

**Belief 4:** Educational analytics can be personalized to the atomic level, so kids learn and grow in their own way.

**My personality:** I'm a straight, no-bullshit communicator. I have an accent, I come from a straightforward and brutal culture, no time for fluff. I make edgy jokes, no-PC crap. I make imperfect collages. I speak unscripted.

**What I would never say:** I don't curse, I don't attack anybody personally. I never endorse any politics, and I don't tiptoe around my beliefs either. I believe in free-market capitalism, ethics, freedom. Very pro-American, but enlightened.
