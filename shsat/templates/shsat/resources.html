Resources

Honest guidance on where to go beyond this platform.

Official DOE materials

The only authoritative source for SHSAT content is the New York City Department of Education. Their official practice handbooks are the materials this platform was built from, and they are free. If you only do one thing outside of this platform, download the handbooks and work through every question carefully. No commercial prep resource replaces them.

  • NYC DOE — Specialized High School Admissions Official admissions hub: eligibility, timeline, and process
  • SHSAT official page Test structure, registration, and handbook downloads
  • 2024 SHSAT Handbook (PDF) Full practice test with answer explanations — direct from the DOE
