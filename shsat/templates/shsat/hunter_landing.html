Why we built this

Most test prep is built for a student who doesn't exist.

Generic study plans assume every child has the same gaps. They don't. One child loses points on inference traps. Another on quantitative reasoning. A third understands the reading but runs out of time on math. A prep plan that treats them identically wastes everyone's time.

I built this platform because I believe anything can be reverse-engineered to first principles — including a standardized test. So I took apart the Hunter entrance exam question by question, trap by trap, and built practice tests that mirror the real thing. Then I built the analytics to show you exactly where your child's reasoning breaks down, so you stop studying blind and start working on what actually matters.

The Hunter exam will come and go. What stays is the ability to think clearly under pressure, to see through a wrong answer that looks right, and to reason carefully when it counts. That's what this platform actually teaches.
