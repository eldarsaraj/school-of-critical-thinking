About the Hunter exam

The Hunter College High School entrance examination is offered to NYC public school students in Grade 6. It consists of Reading Comprehension, Writing (essay), Quantitative Reasoning, and Math Achievement sections. Unlike the SHSAT, there is no official practice handbook from the school — preparation relies on released materials and reconstructed tests.

Exam structure

The exam is taken in one sitting. Based on past administrations, it includes:

  • Reading Comprehension Multiple passages with comprehension questions
  • Writing One essay prompt; scored separately by graders
  • Quantitative Reasoning Logic and reasoning questions, some with minimal computation
  • Math Achievement Grade-level math questions, harder than standard curriculum
