Built for tutors who want the precision data, not just the score.

We give you precise feedback on your students' progress so you can direct your teaching efforts where they'll be most effective.

Decades of research in education show that the strongest predictor of students' success is precise feedback on items they struggle with the most.

Unlike most test-prep platforms, we dig deep into student test data. We don't only report the areas they struggle with, but we identify each answer choice trap they fall into. The feedback is based on careful, scientific, and human reverse-engineering of each test, and precise identification of the entire distribution of answer choice distractors.

How it works

You assign your students our tests, they complete them at your timeline, and you get a precise report on their attempt: a full dashboard with granular data about correct and incorrect answers, time taken per each section and question, as well as a precise error analysis, showing exactly which traps caught them the most, suggesting areas for improvement. You can share these with your students, their parents, and use them for your own prepping efforts.

According to research, this is the only way students will improve. And the more they get better, the more your reputation and monthly sign-ups grow.

Each of your students will have a full Baseline test (so you can establish the starting point) and four full Benchmark tests, so you can track their progress over time. In addition to these, they will have access to a battery of five Focus sets, containing additional ELA and math questions that will help them hammer out those weak skills.

Good to know

What exams are covered? SHSAT and Hunter, on separate tabs. SHSAT has 1 Baseline + 4 Benchmarks; Hunter has 1 Baseline + 2 Benchmarks. Both included, one account.

How do students join? You get a unique invite code. Students enter it during signup and appear on your dashboard instantly.

What do parents see? Parents get their own login with the same dashboard — scores, error analysis, everything. You look professional without extra work.

Is student data private? Each student's data is visible only to you and their parent. Students in your group cannot see each other's results.

Behind this platform are years of experience in education, analytics, and upbringing of a child in New York City. I'm a father with a kid whose prepping for SHSAT too, and I'm using this exact platform to prepare her for it. I am also a professional academic (I'm a full-time professor of critical thinking at CUNY) passionate about data mining and analytics. I took the entire SHSAT apart (1,368 questions from 12 official DOE sample tests) and identified 28 traps across both ELA and math section. Every wrong answer in Baseline and Benchmark tests maps to one, and you'll get to see each of them on your student report.

I'm not a tutor; I'm not competing with you. I'm giving you a precise diagnostic layer underneath how you already teach.
