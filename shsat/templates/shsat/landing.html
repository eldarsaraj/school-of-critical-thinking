Why we built this

A mistake should never be wasted.

A wrong answer tells you exactly how a student is thinking — and that's something a raw score never will. That conviction is the reason this platform exists.

Our method works from both ends. Before your child ever sits down, our analytics take the exam apart: statistical and linguistic analysis of every question and reading passage, so we know what each item is really measuring and where it buries its traps. After the test, the error analysis goes to work on the mistakes — tracing each one back to the specific reasoning that produced it.

The whole point of preparing for a test like the SHSAT is to learn the reasoning skills that will serve your child for life. The test will come and go. What remains is the ability to think clearly, reason carefully, and make good decisions under pressure. That's what we actually teach.
