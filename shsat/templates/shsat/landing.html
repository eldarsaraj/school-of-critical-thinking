Here's why we're different.

01

Our scores are deliberately conservative

We're not afraid of telling you that your child sucks at reading comprehension. It's better you hear it from us than see the results in November. That's why we never inflate our scores. Every scaled score on this platform is calibrated to under-promise. If your child hits a cutoff here, they'll hit it on test day.

02

No AI chatbot feedback. We're human (imperfectly so)

Every ambitious fool can hook up an API and create a series of SHSAT-sounding practice questions, and "review lessons." We don't do that. Our tests have been created using tools of psychometrics and educational measurement, and they're designed by a person, during one long hot NYC summer.

03

Reverse-engineered from the actual test

Before we even wrote one question, we analyzed 1,368 questions from eight years of DOE-released sample tests. Why? Because that's what we do (and because we're slightly OCD). Every test question here is built from that data: same reading levels, same skill distributions, same trap types. There's no guesswork here; only statistics (and a lot of coffee).

04

If you want a shortcut, this isn't it

If you want a quick solution to your test problem, look elsewhere. You must know that there are no tricks to game the SHSAT, no matter what anybody tells you. There is only hard work, both by you (the parent or a tutor) and by your child (or student). This platform makes it a bit easier by telling you exactly where to focus most of your efforts.

Why we built this

Honestly, a lot of test prep out there is just bullshit.

Let's be honest here: most test prep is just a way to make money off of stressed parents and students. When these gigantic test-prep companies sell their expertise by saying how X% of their students have gotten offers from Stuyvesant or Bronx Science, they're counting on your ignorance of statistics: even if that is true, you can't really tell if those kids got in because of the prep or because of their natural ability. This is called selection bias. Smart parents will know that and be cautious before they spend their entire vacation saving for a week of test prep (in case you haven't noticed, test prep is waaaaaay too expensive).

We don't do that. We focus on helping your child improve. We give you information and intelligence. What you choose to do with that is up to you.
