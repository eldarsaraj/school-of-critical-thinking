The Set Method

ELA is one operation, applied 57 times

SHSAT Reading Comprehension doesn't test reading. It tests a single, learnable operation: deciding whether a statement belongs to a precisely defined set of meaning — the claims the passage explicitly makes.

Once a student understands this, the four types of wrong answer become visible as distinct categories rather than vague feelings. Elimination stops being intuition. It becomes a procedure.

The four traps

TrapWhat it looks like
Out of scopeA claim the passage never addresses — plausible but absent
True but off-targetA real detail from the passage that doesn't answer this specific question
PartialHalf the claim is supported; the other half isn't
ReversalThe passage says the opposite — cause and effect swapped, or a relationship inverted

Every wrong ELA answer in the official DOE handbooks falls into one of these four types. The error analysis tags each wrong answer you select so you can see your pattern, not just your score.
