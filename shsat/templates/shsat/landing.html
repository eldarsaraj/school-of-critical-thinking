The Set Method

A novel approach to cracking reading comprehension

Most students treat SHSAT Reading Comprehension as a reading problem. It is not. It is a classification problem. We offer a cutting-edge approach, the Set Method, which reframes every question as a single learnable operation: deciding whether a statement belongs to a precisely defined set of meaning, the claims the passage explicitly makes.

Once a student internalizes this, wrong answers stop feeling vague and start looking like what they are: predictable, nameable traps. Elimination stops being intuition and becomes a procedure.
